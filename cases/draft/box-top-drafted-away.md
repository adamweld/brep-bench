The box from the origin to (30, 10, 10). Draft its four sides by 30°, with the pull
direction +z and the neutral plane z = 0, the plane of its floor: each side turns about its bottom
edge until it leans 30° from the z direction, inward as it rises.

The two long sides, y = 0 and y = 10, then meet at height 5/tan 30° = 5√3 ≈ 8.660, below the top:
the draft consumes the top face. The solid is the box cut by the four drafted planes, each keeping
the side of it that holds the box's middle; the top plane, z = 10, cuts nothing off.

That is a hip roof with five faces: the floor, the rectangle from (0, 0, 0) to (30, 10, 0); two
trapezoids, from the long edges of the floor to the ridge, the line from (5, 5, 5√3) to
(25, 5, 5√3); and two triangles, from the short edges of the floor to the ridge's ends.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,s \le x \le 30 - s,\ s \le y \le 10 - s,\ 0 \le z \le 10\,\}, \quad s = z\tan 30^\circ$$
