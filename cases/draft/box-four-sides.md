The box from the origin to (20, 12, 10). Draft its four sides by 5°, with the pull direction
+z and the neutral plane z = 0, the plane of its floor: each side turns about the line where it
meets z = 0 until it leans 5° from the z direction, inward as it rises. The floor and the top are
not drafted; the top is trimmed to the drafted sides, and the sides meet one another along new
edges, where their planes cross.

The result is a frustum of a rectangular pyramid, with six faces. Its floor is the rectangle from
(0, 0, 0) to (20, 12, 0), and its section at height z is that rectangle inset z·tan 5° on every
side, so its top is inset 10·tan 5° ≈ 0.875.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,s \le x \le 20 - s,\ s \le y \le 12 - s,\ 0 \le z \le 10\,\}, \quad s = z\tan 5^\circ$$
