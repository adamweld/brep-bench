The box from the origin to (20, 12, 10). Draft one of its sides, x = 20, by 10°, with the
pull direction +z and the neutral plane z = 0: the side turns about its bottom edge, the line
x = 20, z = 0, until it leans 10° from the z direction, inward (toward −x) as it rises. No other
face is drafted. The top and the sides y = 0 and y = 12 are trimmed to the drafted side.

The result is a prism along y. Its section in any plane y = const is the trapezoid through (0, 0),
(20, 0), (20 − 10·tan 10°, 10) and (0, 10), each given as (x, z), and it runs from y = 0 to
y = 12.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,0 \le x \le 20 - z\tan 10^\circ,\ 0 \le y \le 12,\ 0 \le z \le 10\,\}$$
