Two elliptic cones joined base to base, as a ruled loft from a point through an ellipse to a second point, both points on the ellipse's axis.

The ellipse lies in the plane z = 0, centered on the origin, with semi-axis 4 along x and 2 along y. The two points are (0, 0, 3) and (0, 0, −3). Each point is joined to every point of the ellipse by a straight line. The solid has two conical faces and no flat face. Its volume is 16π, but the area of an elliptic cone is an elliptic integral.
