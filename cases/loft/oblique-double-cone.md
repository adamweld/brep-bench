Two oblique cones joined base to base, as a ruled loft from a point through a circle to a second point, both points displaced sideways from the circle's axis.

The circle has radius 3, lies in the plane z = 0 and is centered on the z axis. The two points are (2, 0, −4) below it and (−1, 2, 4) above it. Each point is joined to every point of the circle by a straight line. The solid has two conical faces and no flat face. Its volume is 24π, twice that of a right cone on the circle of height 4, but each cone's area is an elliptic integral.
