A circular cone whose apex is not over the centre of its base, as a ruled loft from a circle to a single point, the solid closed over the circle by a flat disc.

The circle has radius 3, lies in the plane z = 0 and is centered on the z axis. The point is (2, 0, 4), above the circle's disc and off its axis. The point is joined to every point of the circle by a straight line. The solid has one conical face and one flat disc. Its volume is ⅓·9π·4 = 12π, whatever the sideways position of the point, but the area of an oblique cone is an elliptic integral.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,(1-s)\,(x_0, y_0, 0) + s\,(2, 0, 4) : x_0^2 + y_0^2 \le 9,\ 0 \le s \le 1\,\}$$
