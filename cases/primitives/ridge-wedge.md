A wedge whose top is a line. Its base is the rectangle x from 0 to 10, z from 0 to 6, at y = 0. Its top is the line from (0, 4, 3) to (10, 4, 3), a ridge along x above the middle of the base. The two long slopes run from the base's long edges up to the ridge, and the two ends are isosceles triangles: five faces in all.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,0 \le x \le 10,\ 0 \le y \le 4,\ |z - 3| \le 3\,(1 - y/4)\,\}$$
