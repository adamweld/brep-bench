Three quarters of a cone. In the xz plane, take the right triangle with corners (0, 0), (6, 0) and (0, 6). Revolve it 270° about the z axis, right-handed: from +x toward +y. The solid has its base disc sector on z = 0, its apex at (0, 0, 6), and two flat triangular faces, in the xz plane and the yz plane.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,0 \le \rho \le 6\,(1 - z/6),\ 0 \le \varphi \le 270^\circ,\ 0 \le z \le 6\,\}$$
