A small square prism, 0.0015 square about the z axis and 0.0015 tall from z = 0, and a copy a
hundredth wider, 0.001515 square, about the same axis between the same two planes.

Take the copy less the prism: a square tube 0.0015 tall whose walls are 0.0000075 thick, a
two-hundredth of its height, its ends in the planes the prism's own ends lie in.
