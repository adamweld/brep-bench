A small cylinder, of radius 0.00075 and height 0.0015 about the z axis from z = 0, and a copy a
hundredth wider, of radius 0.0007575, on the same axis between the same two planes.

Take the copy less the cylinder: a tube 0.0015 tall whose wall is 0.0000075 thick, a two-hundredth
of its height, its ends in the planes the cylinder's own ends lie in.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,r^2 \le x^2 + y^2 \le R^2,\ 0 \le z \le h\,\}$$
