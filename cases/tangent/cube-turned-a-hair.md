A cube of side 10 from (−5, −5, 0) to (5, 5, 10), united with a copy of itself turned 0.001°
about the z axis.

The union is a prism 10 high over the union of the two squares, a sixteen-sided polygon: each
square's corners stand out past the other's sides by up to 8.7e-5, and each square's side
crosses the other's at 0.001°, about 4.4e-5 from its middle. The tops lie in one plane and so do
the bottoms; the sides do not. The union is larger than the cube by 8.7e-6 of its volume and of
its area, more than a pass allows: a kernel that takes the turned cube's sides for the cube's
own does not pass.

**As a set:**

$$Q \cup R_z(0.001^\circ)\,Q, \quad Q = [-5,5]^2\times[0,10]$$
