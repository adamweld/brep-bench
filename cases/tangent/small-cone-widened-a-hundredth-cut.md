A small cone, its base of radius 0.00075 at z = 0 and its apex 0.0015 above it on the z axis, and
a copy whose base is a hundredth wider, radius 0.0007575, with the same base plane and apex.

Take the copy less the cone: a conical shell 0.0000075 thick at its base, thinning to nothing at
the apex the two share.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,r\left(1 - \tfrac{z}{h}\right) \le \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \le R\left(1 - \tfrac{z}{h}\right),\ 0 \le z \le h\,\}$$
