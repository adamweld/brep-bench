A cylinder of radius 5 and height 10 about the z axis from z = 0, less a cylinder of radius 4.999
on the same axis between the same two planes.

What is left is a tube 10 tall whose wall is 0.001 thick, its ends two thin annuli in the planes
both cylinders' ends lie in.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,r^2 \le x^2 + y^2 \le R^2,\ 0 \le z \le h\,\}$$
