A small cylinder, of radius 0.00075 and height 0.0015 about the z axis from z = 0, and a copy of
it raised by 0.000015 along z, a hundredth of its height. The copy's side lies in the cylinder's
own side.

Take their common part: the cylinder from z = 0.000015 to z = 0.0015. Its side lies in both
sides, and its ends are the copy's bottom and the cylinder's own top.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 0.00075^2,\ 0.000015 \le z \le 0.0015\,\}$$
