A cone, its base of radius 5 at z = 0 and its apex 10 above it on the z axis, less a cone of base
radius 4.999 with the same base plane and the same apex.

What is left is a conical shell 0.001 thick at its base, thinning to nothing at the apex the two
share, its base a thin annulus.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,r\left(1 - \tfrac{z}{h}\right) \le \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \le R\left(1 - \tfrac{z}{h}\right),\ 0 \le z \le h\,\}$$
