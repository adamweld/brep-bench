A ball of radius 5 centered on the origin, kept only where z is between −3 and 4 and x is not negative: cut by the three planes z = −3, z = 4 and x = 0. Half of a thick slice of the ball is left: a zone of the sphere, two flat half discs, of radius 4 at the bottom and radius 3 at the top, and the cut through the middle in the plane x = 0.

**As a set:**

$$B_5 \cap \{\,x \ge 0,\ -3 \le z \le 4\,\}$$
