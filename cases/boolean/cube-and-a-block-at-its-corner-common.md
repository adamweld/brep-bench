A cube and a block at its corner, flush at three faces, their common part

A cube from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 10, 10) and a block from (0, 0, 0) to (7, 4, 5). Make the part common to both.

The block lies wholly in the cube, and its faces at x = 0, y = 0 and z = 0 lie in the cube's. The common part is the block.

**As a set:**

$$[0,10]^3 \cap [0,7]\times[0,4]\times[0,5]$$
