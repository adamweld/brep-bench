A block and an identical block, their common part

Two blocks, each from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 8, 6), one exactly on the other. Make the part common to both.

Every face of one lies in a face of the other, so the common part is the block itself.

**As a set:**

$$[0,10]\times[0,8]\times[0,6]$$
