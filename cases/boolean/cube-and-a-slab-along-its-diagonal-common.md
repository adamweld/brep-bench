A cube and a slab along its diagonal, one long side on it, their common part

A cube from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 10, 10) and a slab from z = 0 to z = 10 whose footprint is the rectangle with corners (0, 0), (10, 10), (12.5, 7.5) and (2.5, −2.5): it is 10√2 long, as long as the cube's diagonal, and 5/√2 wide, one long side along the diagonal of the cube's bottom from the corner (0, 0) to the corner (10, 10), its ends square to the diagonal and through those corners. Make the part common to both.

The slab's other long side is the plane x − y = 5. The common part is the prism 10 high on the trapezoid (0, 0), (5, 0), (10, 5), (10, 10), of area 37.5.

**As a set:**

$$[0,10]^3 \cap \{\,0 \le x + y \le 20,\ 0 \le x - y \le 5\,\}$$
