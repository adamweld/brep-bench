A cube and a block shifted down through its bottom, their common part

A cube from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 10, 10) and a block from (0, 0, −4) to (10, 6, 5). Make the part common to both.

The block's x extent is the cube's and its face at y = 0 lies in the cube's; it overhangs the cube's bottom by 4 and ends at z = 5 inside it. The common part is the block from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 6, 5).

**As a set:**

$$[0,10]^3 \cap [0,10]\times[0,6]\times[-4,5]$$
