Two solid tori about the z axis, each with a middle circle of radius 5 and a tube of radius 2.
The first middle circle lies in z = −1, the second in z = 1. Make their common part.

In a half-plane through the z axis, the common section is the lens of two disks of radius 2,
centered at distance 5 from the axis and at heights −1 and 1. Revolving this lens leaves a solid
ring bounded by portions of both tori. Their surfaces meet in two separate circles in z = 0,
of radii 5 − √3 and 5 + √3; both circles bound the result. The solid lies between z = −1 and
z = 1 and does not reach the axis.

The lens has area L = 8π/3 − 2√3 and perimeter P = 8π/3. The centroids of both its area and its
boundary lie at radius 5 by symmetry. Pappus's theorems therefore give volume 2π·5·L and area
2π·5·P. The angles in these formulas are in radians.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,(\rho - 5)^2 + (z+1)^2 \le 2^2\,\} \cap \{\,(\rho - 5)^2 + (z-1)^2 \le 2^2\,\}$$
