A plate, the cylinder of radius 8 on the z axis from z = −6 to z = 0, with a ball of radius 5
centered on the origin standing in it: the ball's upper half rises from the plate's top face. The
ball meets that face in the circle of radius 5 at z = 0, a concave edge. Chamfer it by a distance
and an angle: 1 out from the edge on the plate's top, the chamfer making 50° with the plate. A
concave chamfer adds material: it fills the groove between the plate and the ball.

In the plane through the z axis the plate's top is the straight line z = 0 from the edge out to
the plate's rim. The chamfer starts on it at (6.0, 0), 1 out from the edge, and is the straight
line from there that rises at 50° back toward the ball until it meets it, 1.888 along, at
Q ≈ (4.786, 1.447). The chamfer is the cone that line sweeps about the z axis, and the solid is
the plate and the ball with the ring between them and that cone filled in.

The angle is measured from the plate's top, which is flat.
