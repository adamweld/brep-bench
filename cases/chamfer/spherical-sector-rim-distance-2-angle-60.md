A spherical sector: the part of a ball of radius 5 centered on the origin that lies inside the cone
with its apex at the origin, about the z axis, opening upward with half angle α, cos α = 3/5 (about
53.13°). The cone's side meets the ball in the circle of radius 4 at z = 3, the sector's rim.
Chamfer the rim by a distance and an angle: 2 down the cone from the rim, the chamfer making 60°
with the cone's side.

In the plane through the z axis the cone's side is the straight line from the origin to the rim. The
chamfer starts on it at P, 2 from the rim, (2.4, 1.8) in (ρ, z), and is the straight line
from P that makes 60° with the cone's line continued toward the rim, turned toward the z axis, into
the sector. It runs 2.772 to meet the ball at Q ≈ (2.068, 4.552). The chamfer is the cone
that line sweeps about the z axis, and the solid is the sector cut by it: the cone up to P, the
chamfer from P to Q, and the ball above Q.

The angle is measured from the cone's side, which is straight in that plane, so it is the same
angle everywhere along the chamfer's start.
