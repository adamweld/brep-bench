The part of a ball of radius 5 centered on the origin that lies below the plane z = 3. Its flat
face is the disk of radius 4 at z = 3. Chamfer the disk's rim with two distances: 4 on the flat
face, the whole way to its center, and 1 on the ball.

Each distance is measured in a straight line from the edge, in the plane through the z axis (the
plane square to the edge). On the flat face that reaches the center (0, 0, 3), so the flat face
shrinks to a point. On the ball it reaches the circle whose points are 1 in a straight line from
the rim, the chord 1 long, turned 2·asin(1/10) about the ball's center down from the rim: radius
ρ₁ = 98/25 + 9√11/50 ≈ 4.517 at z₁ = 147/50 − 6√11/25 ≈ 2.144. The chamfer is the cone from that
circle up to its apex at (0, 0, 3), and the solid is the cut ball cut again by it: the ball
below z₁ under that cone.
