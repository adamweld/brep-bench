The upper half of a ball of radius 5 centered on the origin, z ≥ 0. Chamfer the circle where its
flat face z = 0 meets the ball, with two distances: 5 on the flat face, the whole way to its
center, and 1 on the ball.

Each distance is measured in a straight line from the edge, in the plane through the z axis (the
plane square to the edge). On the flat face that reaches the center, so the flat face shrinks to
a point. On the ball it reaches the circle whose points are 1 in a straight line from the rim,
the chord 1 long, at radius 4.9 and height 3√11/10 ≈ 0.995. The chamfer is the cone from the
origin up to that circle, and the solid is the hemisphere cut by it: everything of the ball
inside the cone, a spherical sector whose half angle α from the z axis has sin α = 49/50.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,\lVert p\rVert \le 5,\ 49z \ge 3\sqrt{11}\,\rho\,\}$$
