A ball of radius 5 centered on the origin, with a hole of radius 2 bored right through it along the
z axis. The bore meets the ball in two circles of radius 2, at z = ±√21. Chamfer the upper one
with two distances: 2√21 down the bore, its whole length, and 1 on the ball.

Each distance is measured in a straight line from the edge, in the plane through the z axis (the
plane square to the edge). Down the bore that reaches the lower rim, so the bore shrinks to that
circle and is gone. On the ball it reaches the circle whose points are 1 in a straight line from
the upper rim, the chord 1 long, turned 2·asin(1/10) about the ball's center away from the bore:
radius ρ₁ = 49/25 + 3√231/50 ≈ 2.872 at z₁ = 49√21/50 − 3√11/25 ≈ 4.093. The chamfer is the cone
from the lower rim up to that circle, and the solid is the bored ball cut by it: the ball
between z = −√21 and z₁, with a conical hole widening from radius 2 at the bottom to ρ₁ at the
top.
