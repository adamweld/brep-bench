A puck: the cylinder of radius 6 on the z axis from z = 0 to z = 10 with both its circular edges
rounded to radius 2, each round a quarter of a torus whose tube has radius 2 about the circle of
radius 4 at z = 2 or z = 8. Shrink it by 3: the solid of every point at least 3 from the puck's
outside.

That is more than the rounds' radius. A round moved 3 inward would be a torus of tube radius 2 − 3 =
−1, turned inside out: it is no part of the result, and the moved side and the moved top or bottom
run on past it until they meet in a sharp edge. The result is the plain cylinder of radius 3 from z
= 3 to z = 7, its edges sharp.

**As a set:** the puck is the cylinder of radius 4 from z = 2 to z = 8 grown by 2, so shrinking it
by 3 is shrinking that cylinder by 1.
