A plate, the cylinder of radius 8 about the z axis from z = −2 to z = 0, and on it the ball of radius 3 about
(0, 0, 1), joined: the ball stands up out of the plate's top through the circle of radius √8. The concave edge
where the ball meets the plate's top is filleted to radius 1: a piece of a torus tangent to the top and to the ball. Grow the solid by 2 with
rounded edges: every point within 2 of it.

That is more than the fillet's radius. A fillet grown by its radius or more shrinks to nothing: its
moved face would be a torus of tube radius 1 − 2 ≤ 0, and the moved top and the moved ball run on until they meet
in a sharp concave edge, as if the fillet had never been there. So the result is the sharp plate and ball
grown by 2: the bottom moved down 2, the plate's side out 2 with a quarter round of radius 2 at each of its
rims, the plate's top up 2 and the ball grown to radius 5 about (0, 0, 1), meeting in the circle of radius
√((3 + d)² − (d − 1)²) = 4.89897948557 at z = 2.

**As a set:** the filleted solid lies between the sharp one and the sharp one closed by 1 (grown by 1 and shrunk
back), and growing either of those by at least 1 gives the same solid, so the result is the sharp plate and ball grown
by 2: every point within 2 of it.
