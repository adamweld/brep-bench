A plate, the box from (−15, −15, 0) to (15, 15, 4), with a hole punched through it by a tilted box
pushed aslant wholly through it.

The box is 4 × 3 × 2, its edges along the orthonormal directions e₁ = (2, 1, 2)/3, e₂ = (−2, 2, 1)/3
and e₃ = (1, 2, −2)/3: everything (−2, −1, −8) + a e₁ + b e₂ + c e₃ with |a| ≤ 2, |b| ≤ 1.5 and |c|
≤ 1. As placed it is wholly below the plate.

It slides without turning along the vector (4, 2, 18), ending centered on (2, 1, 10), wholly above
the plate. Cut from the plate everything the box passes through.

No face or edge of the box is level or upright: where it passes, its silhouette turns at its
corners. Each level of the plate loses the box's whole shadow along the push, a hexagon, so the hole
is an oblique hexagonal prism.
