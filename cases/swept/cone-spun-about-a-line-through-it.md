The solid a cone fills as it is spun partway about a line that crosses its axis aslant, inside it.

The cone's axis is the line through the origin along w = (cos 30°, 0, sin 30°), 60° from the z axis.
Its point is at −2w and it opens along +w at 50° (each side 25° from its axis) to a flat base at
+5w, of radius 7 tan 25°. The z axis meets its axis at the origin, inside it.

It turns rigidly about the z axis, right-handed (from +x toward +y), by 150°. Make the solid of
everything the cone passes through.

The z axis runs through the cone, leaving it through its side above and below the origin. There the
turn holds the cone's side still, and the contact passes over those two points as well as down to
the cone's point.
