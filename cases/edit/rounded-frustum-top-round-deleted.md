A cone frustum on the z axis, from the disk of radius 6 at z = 0 to the disk of radius 3 at z = 6 (its side the cone
2r + z = 12), with both its rims rounded to radius 3/2: each round a piece of a torus of tube radius 3/2 tangent to the
end and to the cone.

Then edit it directly: delete the top round only. Deleting a face takes it out of the solid and closes the gap it leaves with the
faces round it, each run on along its own surface until they meet. The top runs on outward and the cone up until they meet in the circle of radius 3 at z = 6; the base's round stays.

The result is the frustum with only its base rim rounded.
