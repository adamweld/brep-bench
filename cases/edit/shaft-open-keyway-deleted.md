A shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, with an open keyway cut
into it: the box −1 ≤ x ≤ 1, y ≤ −3, z ≥ 8 cut away, so the keyway is 2 wide, its floor 3 from the
axis, and it runs from z = 8 out through the shaft's top end. It has four faces: its floor (the
plane y = −3), its two walls (the planes x = −1 and x = 1) and its end (the plane z = 8).

Then edit the shaft directly: delete all four of the keyway's faces. Deleting faces takes them out
of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them, each run on along its own
surface until they meet. The faces round the keyway are the shaft's cylinder and its top end, so the
cylinder closes over it and the top grows back to a whole disc.

The result is the plain shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 20, with three faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 25,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
