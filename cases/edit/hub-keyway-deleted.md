A hub: the tube about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, outer radius 10, its bore of radius 4, with a
keyway cut along the bore's whole length: the box −1 ≤ x ≤ 1, −5 ≤ y ≤ 0 cut away, so the keyway is
2 wide and its floor is the plane y = −5. It has three faces, its floor and its two walls (the
planes x = −1 and x = 1), each running from one end of the hub to the other.

Then edit the hub directly: delete the keyway's three faces. Deleting faces takes them out of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them,
each run on along its own surface until they meet. The faces round the keyway are the
bore and the hub's two ends, so the bore closes over it and the ends grow back to whole rings.

The result is the plain hub, with four faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,16 \le x^2 + y^2 \le 100,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
