The half of the ring torus about the z axis whose tube has radius 2 about the circle of radius 6 that lies above
the plane z = 0: its flat face the ring 4 ≤ r ≤ 8 at z = 0. Its outer rim, the circle of radius 8 at z = 0, rounded to radius 1/2: a piece of a torus whose tube has radius 1/2 about the circle of radius 6 + √2 at z = 1/2, tangent to the flat and to the half torus.

Then edit it directly: delete the round. Deleting a face takes it out of the solid and closes the gap it leaves
with the faces round it, each run on along its own surface until they meet. The flat runs on outward and the torus
down until they meet in the circle of radius 8 at z = 0.

The result is the half torus.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} - 6)^2 + z^2 \le 4,\ z \ge 0\,\}$$
