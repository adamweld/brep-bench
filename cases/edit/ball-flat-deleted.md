A ball of radius 5 about the origin with its top cut flat: everything above z = 3 cut away, leaving
a flat disc of radius 4 at z = 3.

Then edit the ball directly: delete the flat. Deleting faces takes them out of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them,
each run on along its own surface until they meet. The flat's whole edge borders the ball, so the
ball closes over it.

The result is the plain ball of radius 5.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \le 25\,\}$$
