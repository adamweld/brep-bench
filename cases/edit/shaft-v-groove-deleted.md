A shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, with a V-groove turned
into it: everything farther from the axis than 3 + |z − 10| cut away, so the groove's two faces are
cones meeting at radius 3 at z = 10, the lower running out to the shaft at z = 8 and the upper at
z = 12.

Then edit the shaft directly: delete both of the groove's cones together. Deleting faces takes them out of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them,
each run on along its own surface until they meet. The faces
round the groove are the shaft's cylinder below it and above it, which are one surface, so that
cylinder runs on across the groove and the two become one face again.

The result is the plain shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 20, with three faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 25,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
