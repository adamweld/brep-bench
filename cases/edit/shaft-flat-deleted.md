A shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, with a flat milled along
its whole length: everything with y < −4 cut away. The flat is one face, the strip of the plane
y = −4 from x = −3 to x = 3, running from one end of the shaft to the other.

Then edit the shaft directly: delete the flat. Deleting a face takes it out of the solid and closes
the gap it leaves with the faces round it, each run on along its own surface until they meet. The
flat's two long sides both border the shaft's cylinder, and its two short sides the shaft's ends, so
the cylinder closes over it and the ends grow back to whole discs.

The result is the plain shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 20, with three faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 25,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
