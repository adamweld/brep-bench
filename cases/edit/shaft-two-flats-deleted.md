A shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, with two opposite flats
milled along its whole length: everything with |x| > 4 cut away. Each flat is a strip of a plane,
x = 4 and x = −4, from y = −3 to y = 3, running from one end of the shaft to the other, and the
shaft's cylinder is left in two pieces between them.

Then edit the shaft directly: delete both flats together. Deleting faces takes them out of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them,
each run on along its own surface until they meet. Each flat borders the cylinder on
both long sides and the shaft's ends at its short ones, so the cylinder closes over both and the
ends grow back to whole discs.

The result is the plain shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 20, with three faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 25,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
