A shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 20, with a round-bottomed
groove turned into it: the torus about the z axis whose tube, of radius 1.5, runs round the circle of
radius 5 at z = 10 is cut away. The groove is one face, the inner half of that torus, from z = 8.5 to
z = 11.5, down to radius 3.5 at z = 10.

Then edit the shaft directly: delete the groove's face. Deleting faces takes them out of the solid and closes the gap they leave with the faces round them,
each run on along its own surface until they meet. The faces round the groove are
the shaft's cylinder below it and above it, which are one surface, so that cylinder runs on across
the groove and the two become one face again.

The result is the plain shaft, the cylinder of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 20, with three faces.

**As a set:**

$$\{\,x^2 + y^2 \le 25,\ 0 \le z \le 20\,\}$$
