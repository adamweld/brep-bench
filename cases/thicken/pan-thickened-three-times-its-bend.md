A sheet of revolution about the z axis, a pan with a tight bend: a flat floor, the disk of radius 5
in the plane z = 0; a bend, the quarter of a torus whose tube has radius 1 about the circle of
radius 5 at z = 1, from the floor's edge out and up to the circle of radius 6 at z = 1; and a wall,
the cylinder of radius 6 from z = 1 up to z = 8. Its faces meet tangentially. In the half-plane
through the axis, with (ρ, z) the distance from the axis and the height, it is the line from (0, 0)
to (5, 0), the quarter circle of radius 1 about (5, 1) to (6, 1), and the line up to (6, 8), turned
a whole turn.

Thicken it by 3 to the inside, up from the floor and in from the wall. The thickened sheet is every
point p + s·n, for p on the sheet, n its unit normal on that side, and s from 0 to 3.

That is more than the bend's radius, 1. The bend's face, moved 3 toward its tube's center line,
would pass through it and turn inside out, a torus of tube radius −2; all of it lies inside the
solid and is no part of its boundary. The floor moved up 3 and the wall moved in 3 meet instead in a
concave edge, the circle of radius 3 at z = 3, and the bend fills the quarter disk behind it. In the
half-plane the solid's section is bounded by the sheet's own profile and the polyline from (6, 8)
through (3, 8), (3, 3) and (0, 3), closed down the axis.
