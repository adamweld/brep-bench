A sheet of revolution about the z axis, a cup: a flat floor, the disk of radius 4 in the plane z =
0; a bend, the quarter of a torus whose tube has radius 2 about the circle of radius 4 at z = 2,
from the floor's edge out and up to the circle of radius 6 at z = 2; and a wall, the cylinder of
radius 6 from z = 2 up to z = 10. Its faces meet tangentially. In the half-plane through the axis,
with (ρ, z) the distance from the axis and the height, it is the line from (0, 0) to (4, 0), the
quarter circle of radius 2 about (4, 2) to (6, 2), and the line up to (6, 10), turned a whole turn.

Thicken it by 3 to the inside, up from the floor and in from the wall. The thickened sheet is every
point p + s·n, for p on the sheet, n its unit normal on that side, and s from 0 to 3.

That is more than the bend's radius, 2. The bend's face, moved 3 toward its tube's center line,
would pass through it and turn inside out, a torus of tube radius −1; all of it lies inside the
solid and is no part of its boundary. The floor moved up 3 and the wall moved in 3 meet instead in a
concave edge, the circle of radius 3 at z = 3, and the bend fills the quarter disk behind it. In the
half-plane the solid's section is bounded by the sheet's own profile and the polyline from (6, 10)
through (3, 10), (3, 3) and (0, 3), closed down the axis.
